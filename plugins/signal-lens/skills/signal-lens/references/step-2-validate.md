# Step 2 — Validate the problem

**A signal is someone already doing something about a problem.** If nobody is doing
anything yet there is nothing to find, and any list you produce will be invented.

Grade what you can evidence, from the profile's `workarounds` and `competitors`:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Money already moving** | someone is paid for this today — a tool, a firm, a freelancer, a hire |
| **Effort already spent** | a workaround exists: the spreadsheet, the manual process |
| **A consequence with a number** | hours lost, money leaked, a date missed |
| **Complaint only** | people say it is annoying. Nobody pays, nobody works around it |
| **The seller's word only** | the problem appears in their marketing and nowhere else |

The top three are past behaviour. The bottom two are opinion about a hypothetical.

**Say which row you reached, in one line, even when the answer is obvious.** *"People
already pay CPA firms two to three thousand a month to do this by hand"* is what makes
everything after it credible.

**Refuse at the bottom two.** Say the problem is not evidenced outside their own
marketing, show which rows you could and could not fill, name what would change your
mind, and stop. A plausible keyword list for a problem nobody has yet costs a company
months.

If the evidence is thin but real — complaints exist, nobody pays — continue and mark
every signal derived from it as unvalidated.
