# Step 5 — Name the lead, which is often not the poster

Getting this wrong wastes the signal even when detection is perfect. Check
`seller.motion` in the profile before deciding: `dual` changes the answer for most
signals.

- **The poster** — the default.
- **The employer, not the individual** — where a company pays for what a person
  needs. They post; someone else signs. This is what `motion: dual` means, and in
  that case an employee's complaint is a lead for their employer and never for them.
- **A third party in the thread** — the me-too commenter who volunteers their own
  scale under someone else's rant has self-selected into a pain description they did
  not have to write. A thread is a container of leads, not one lead.
- **The channel, not a buyer** — whoever keeps answering recommendation threads.
  Pitching them is the fastest way to lose them. Their doppelgänger is the paid
  affiliate: a link, a coupon code, or suspiciously consistent loyalty.
- **The person who inherits it** — where the signal is a continuity break, the lead
  is whoever picks up the workaround, not whoever is leaving.
- **Nobody.** Some signals identify a company with no contactable person, and some
  identify a person you must not contact. **"No lead" is a valid outcome** — return
  it rather than manufacturing one.

In B2B, name both roles from `seller.buyer` where they differ: the one who feels it
and the one who signs. A signal that reaches only the first is a signal that stalls.
